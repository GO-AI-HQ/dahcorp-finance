# Finance Autonomy and Approval Policy

Default progression is Shadow → paper/simulated → Confirm Every Trade → explicitly bounded autonomy. Each strategy/account/ticker/action type has its own ceiling.

No model can promote itself. Live execution requires hard allowlist, eligible account, available cash after reserve, fresh quote/holdings, notional/exposure/risk limits, single-use preview, configured human/authority confirmation and broker reconciliation.

Grok's future tactical-executor concept is a target role, not current permission.
