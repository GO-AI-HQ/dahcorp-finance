# Finance Authority Hierarchy

1. Explicit investor mandate/approved execution mode.
2. Broker account truth and execution capability.
3. Deterministic risk/policy/hard allowlists and reserve constraints.
4. Fresh execution-authoritative cash/holdings/quote immediately before order.
5. Durable Portfolio/Market/Intelligence state within freshness policy.
6. Strategy Lab reconciliation.
7. OpenAI/Claude/Grok/Perplexity reasoning.
8. Retained/planning evidence, heuristics and hypotheses.

No lower layer may widen a higher authority.
