# Product Mission — DAHCorp Finance

DAHCorp Finance is a private agentic capital-management operating system for one investor. Its central objective is to build a self-funding portfolio whose cash flows increasingly purchase assets without external deposits, while preserving transparent risk, provenance and human control.

The product answers: **What should I do with my money right now, why, how much, and what happens if I do it?**

It combines:
- connected Schwab and Robinhood state;
- income/distribution measurement without equating distributions to profit;
- cash-flow efficiency and self-buy progress;
- Growth/Income/Opportunity strategy lanes;
- semiconductor tactical capital recycling;
- Market Intelligence and normalized evidence;
- Strategy/Modeling Lab analysis;
- Shadow evidence and outcome calibration;
- guarded broker preview/confirmation/execution.

The system is not a stock-picker LLM. Models reason inside a deterministic capital and risk frame.
