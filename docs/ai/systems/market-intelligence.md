# Market Intelligence

Evidence fabric combines market, macro, policy, corporate-event and positioning sources. OpenBB became broad primary market/macro/history evidence; Finnhub supplies ticker/reference/company-event and earnings intelligence; primary government lanes include Federal Register and other public disclosures; FMP supplies preferred income distribution history with budget controls.

Each evidence item preserves source, observed time, information latency, freshness and limitations. Missing means UNKNOWN, not neutral.
