# Income Engine

Measures distributions honestly, derives modeled cash-flow efficiency and tracks self-funding milestones. FMP is preferred for distribution history with OpenBB fallback; provider headline yield is not trusted as canonical annualized income. Future declared distributions do not enter trailing history; ROC/tax character stays UNKNOWN without issuer evidence.

Income research may propose ADD/REPLACE/REWEIGHT/HOLD but cannot widen execution allowlists.
